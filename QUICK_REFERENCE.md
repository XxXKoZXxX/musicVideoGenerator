# 🎬 MusicVid Studio - Quick Reference Card

## 🚀 Launch App

```bash
cd C:\Users\User\musicvid-studio
npm start
```

Then open: **http://localhost:3000**

---

## 4️⃣ The Four Steps

### STEP 1: Images
- **Upload**: Click "Add Images" (works in demo!)
- **Record**: Switch to "Webcam Capture" tab
- **Collage**: Create from multiple images
- **Reorder**: Use ↑/↓ buttons to arrange
- **Count**: Shows how many selected

### STEP 2: Audio
- Upload your music track
- Supported: MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC
- Shows file name and path

### STEP 3: Storyline
Pick one:
- **AI-Generated**: Claude creates unique story (needs backend)
- **Templates**: Choose from **15** pre-built stories
- **Custom**: Write your own scenes

### STEP 4: Video Settings
- **Expand** each panel to see options
- **Basic**: Resolution, FPS, format, speed
- **Transitions**: Type, duration, effects
- **Visual**: Colors, brightness, filters
- **Audio**: Volume, normalization, beat sync

## ⚙️ Settings Cheat Sheet

### RESOLUTION
- `480p` - Small/mobile
- `720p` - HD/web
- `1080p` - Full HD (recommended)
- `1440p` - 2K/professional
- `2160p` - 4K/cinema

### FPS (Frame Rate)
- `24` - Cinema/slow
- `30` - Smooth/standard
- `60` - Super smooth/action

### QUALITY
- `Low` - Fast, smaller file
- `Medium` - Balanced
- `High` - Good quality
- `Ultra` - Best quality, large file

### SPEED
- `0.25x` - Slow-motion
- `0.5x` - Half speed
- `1.0x` - Normal (default)
- `1.5x` - Faster
- `2.0x` - Double speed

### TRANSITIONS
- `Fade` - Smooth blend
- `Pan` - Camera movement
- `Zoom` - Growth/shrink
- `Slide` - Horizontal move
- `Wipe` - Directional sweep
- `Cross-Dissolve` - Overlapping
- `Blur` - Fuzzy transition
- `Spin` - Rotating effect

### EFFECTS
- `None` - No effect
- `VHS Tape` - Retro 80s look
- `Glitch` - Digital artifacts
- `Chromatic Aberration` - RGB shift
- `Film Grain` - Classic film
- `Scanlines` - TV scanlines
- `Retro 8-bit` - Pixelated

### FILTERS
- `None` - Original colors
- `Sepia` - Vintage brown
- `B&W Film Noir` - Black & white
- `Cool Blue` - Cold tone
- `Warm Orange` - Warm tone
- `Cyberpunk` - Neon sci-fi
- `Thermal` - Heat camera

### ASPECT RATIO
- `16:9` - YouTube/TV
- `9:16` - TikTok/Reels/Shorts
- `1:1` - Instagram/Square
- `4:3` - Classic/old TV

### FORMAT
- `MP4` - Universal, plays everywhere
- `WebM` - Modern browsers
- `MOV` - Apple/Final Cut
- `AVI` - Windows/legacy

### COLOR SLIDERS
- **Brightness**: 50% (dark) → 150% (bright)
- **Contrast**: 50% (flat) → 150% (sharp)
- **Saturation**: 0% (B&W) → 200% (vivid)

## 🆚 Settings Impact

| Change | Effect | Time Impact |
|--------|--------|------------|
| ↑ Resolution | Sharper image | Slower ⬆️ |
| ↑ FPS | Smoother motion | Slower ⬆️ |

## ✨ Pro Tips

1. **Start with defaults**: Quality=High, 1080p, 30 FPS
2. **Test small**: Use 720p for testing, 1080p final
3. **Match music**: Genre + template should match mood
4. **Go easy on effects**: 1-2 effects usually looks better than many
5. **Check file size**: Don't go over platform limits
6. **Use beat sync**: Makes music videos look pro
7. **Saturate sad songs**: Less = more emotional
8. **Boost upbeat**: More saturation + faster speed
9. **Filters matter**: Right filter can make/break a video
10. **Save settings**: Remember your favorite combinations
