# 🚀 How to Share Astraea with People to Test

Astraea is completely configured so you can share it with friends, family, and beta testers on **iPhone**, **Android**, **Mac**, and **PC** anywhere in the world!

---

## ⚡ Method 1: 1-Click Instant Share Launcher (Recommended)

You can launch a live public HTTPS link anytime from your computer:

1. **Double-click** the file **`SHARE_APP.cmd`** in your project folder.
2. A terminal window will open, connect to the secure Cloudflare tunnel, and generate your live link (e.g., `https://wonder-lobby-chelsea-enters.trycloudflare.com`).
3. **The link is automatically copied to your clipboard!** Just press **`Ctrl + V`** to paste it into text messages, WhatsApp, Discord, or email.
4. Keep the terminal window open while testers are exploring the app.

---

## 📤 Method 2: Share Directly from Inside the App

While you or your testers have the app open:

1. Click the **"Share App"** button in the top navigation bar or the **"Invite Testers & Custom Message"** button on the home dashboard.
2. A sharing modal will open with:
   - **One-Click Copy Link**: Instant public HTTPS link.
   - **Native Share Button**: (On iPhone/Android) Triggers your phone's native share drawer (iMessage, WhatsApp, AirDrop, etc.).
   - **In-Person QR Code**: Have someone point their phone camera at your screen to open the app instantly.
   - **Pre-written Invitation Templates**:
     - *General Tester Invite*
     - *Twin Flame / Compatibility Match Test*
     - *366 Day Archetype & Birthday Fortune*

---

## 📱 How Testers Install It on Their Phone (PWA)

Testers do not need to install anything from the App Store or Google Play:

- **iPhone / iPad (Safari)**: Open the link $\rightarrow$ Tap the **Share** button (box with upward arrow) $\rightarrow$ Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** $\rightarrow$ Tap **Add**.
- **Android (Chrome)**: Open the link $\rightarrow$ Tap the **Menu (3 dots)** $\rightarrow$ Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.

The **Astraea gold star app icon** will appear directly on their phone home screen and open full-screen like a native app.

---

## 🌐 Method 3: Permanent 24/7 Free Cloud Hosting (Optional)

If you want the app to stay online 24/7 even when your computer is shut down, you can deploy the `build/` folder to free hosting in 30 seconds:

### Deploy with Vercel (Free):
```bash
npm install -g vercel
vercel --prod build
```

### Deploy with Netlify (Free):
```bash
npx netlify-cli deploy --prod --dir=build
```
