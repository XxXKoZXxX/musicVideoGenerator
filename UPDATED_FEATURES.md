## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### For Different Music Genres:
- **Pop/Upbeat**: High saturation, zoom transitions, beat sync
- **Indie/Alternative**: Sepia or cool blue filter, fade transitions
- **Electronic/EDM**: Cyberpunk filter, glitch effect, 60 FPS
- **Hip-Hop/Rap**: High contrast, beat sync, zoom effect
- **Sad/Emotional**: Desaturated, slow speed (0.5x), film grain
- **Retro/Nostalgia**: Sepia, VHS effect, scanlines
- **Sci-Fi**: Cyberpunk/thermal filters, chromatic aberration
- **Party/Celebration**: High saturation, cross-dissolve, normal speed

### Export Tips:
- **For YouTube**: Use 1080p, 30 FPS, MP4
- **For TikTok**: Use 9:16 aspect ratio, 720p
- **For Instagram**: Use 1:1 aspect ratio, 1080p
- **For Archival**: Use 2160p (4K), High quality, MOV format
