## 🎬 How to Use (Full Workflow)

### Step by Step

1. **Open App**

   ```bash
   npm start
   ```

2. **Step 1 - Add Images**
   - Click "Add More Images" 3-5 times
   - Demo images load automatically
   - Use ↑/↓ to reorder
   - Use X to delete

3. **Step 2 - Add Audio** (Optional in demo)
   - Click "Select Audio"
   - Choose your music file
   - Or leave empty (demo allows this)

4. **Step 3 - Pick Template**
   - Browse 15 templates
   - Click to see full template details
   - Each has 5 pre-written scenes
   - Or write your own custom story

5. **Step 4 - Configure Video**
   - **Expand** each settings panel
   - Adjust resolution, quality, format
   - Pick transition type & duration
   - Add effects and filters
   - Adjust color (brightness, contrast, saturation)
   - Enable beat sync for music videos

6. **Generate**
   - Click "Generate Video Now"
   - Rendering starts (when backend is ready)
   - Download MP4 to your device

## 🔧 Settings Tips by Genre

### Pop / Upbeat

- High saturation (120%+)
- Zoom or pan transitions
- 30 FPS, normal speed
- Beat sync enabled

### Indie / Alternative

- Lower saturation (85-100%)
- Fade or slide transitions
- Sepia or cool blue filter
- Slower speed (0.9x)

### Electronic / EDM

- Cyberpunk or thermal filter
- Zoom transitions with glitch effect
- 60 FPS for smoothness
- Fast speed (1.5x-2.0x)
- Beat sync on

### Hip-Hop / Rap

- High contrast
- Zoom or spin transitions
- Beat sync essential
- Consider glitch effect

### Ballad / Emotional

- Desaturated colors (70%)
- Slow transitions (1.0s+)
- Film grain effect
- Slow speed (0.8x-0.9x)
- B&W or sepia filter

### Cinematic / Epic

- 1440p or 4K resolution
- Pan transitions
- 24 FPS (cinema feel)
- Cubic interpolation
- Motion blur effect
